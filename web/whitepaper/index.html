Vault NFT whitepaper

SATO locked as Stones, seasons, scarcity, and permissionless rewards.

Sato Stones is a 2,100-supply ERC-721A vault collection. Each Stone locks SATO for a chosen period, stores the vault state on-chain, and uses that locked position for seasonal pool distribution and prize-draw eligibility.

No bonding-curve mint 2,100 mints ever 15 / 30 / 60 day locks 100% redeem after lock

1. Overview

The core mechanic is simple: a minter deposits SATO, pays a 2% mint fee into the current season pool, and receives one NFT vault. The remaining SATO becomes peakSato, the escrowed amount tracked by the Stone.

SATO is not sold through a bonding curve during mint. The protocol is designed around locked SATO, fixed supply, seasonal redistribution, and optional early-exit penalties. After the selected lock ends, the owner can redeem all remaining locked SATO and keep the NFT as a historical artifact.

3. Mint, Intake, and Escrow

Minting is single-quantity. The contract measures received SATO by balance delta, so fee-on-transfer behavior is tolerated as long as received SATO is not below the expected escrow threshold.

mintFee = received × 200 / 10,000 peakSato = received - mintFee lockEnd = block.timestamp + selectedLockDays

The vault stores peakSato, satoLocked, lockEnd, weightAtMint, lock duration, rarity, Genesis status, and redemption state. A Stone cannot be re-locked after redeem.

4. Weight, Rarity, and Genesis

Weight is fixed at mint and does not change later. It is used for season rewards and lottery tickets while the vault remains active and locked.

weightAtMint = sqrt(peakSato) × lockMultiplier × genesisMultiplier

5. Redeem and Early Exit

Once the lock has ended, the owner can call redeem and receive 100% of the remaining locked SATO. The NFT is kept, but the vault becomes empty and is no longer eligible for seasons or lottery.

Before lock end, the owner can call earlyExit. This burns the NFT, returns locked SATO minus a progressive penalty, and does not reopen a mint slot. Partial remaining days are rounded up, so exiting in the final partial day still pays one day of penalty.

daysRemaining = ceil((lockEnd - now) / 1 day) penaltyBps = basePenaltyBps × daysRemaining / lockDays penaltySato = peakSato × penaltyBps / 10,000

6. Seasons and Community Pool

Seasons are global 15-day windows. Mint fees and the pool share of early-exit penalties accumulate into the active season pool. After a season ends, anyone can finalize it.

Finalization scans active locked Stones, records snapshotOwner for each eligible token, sums total weight, computes claim amounts, and marks the season finalized. Claims belong to the snapshot owner, even if the NFT is sold later.

rawClaim = distributable × tokenWeight / totalSeasonWeight walletCap = distributable × 10% if walletRaw > walletCap: paid = rawClaim × walletCap / walletRaw

Unallocated SATO from zero-weight seasons, rounding, or wallet-cap scaling rolls into undistributedCarry for the next season.

7. Lottery and VRF Prize Draws

The prize fund receives 15% of each early-exit penalty. When the fund reaches the configured minimum and the draw interval has elapsed, anyone can request a prize draw.

Draw tickets are snapshotted at request time: active locked Stones contribute weightAtMint. The winner is selected from that frozen ticket set after Chainlink VRF v2.5 returns randomness. This prevents post-request transfers or exits from changing the winner universe.

Request`requestPrizeDraw()` snapshots tickets and asks VRF for randomness.
Assign`rawFulfillRandomWords()` assigns the prize to the snapshotted owner.
ClaimWinner pulls SATO through `claimPrize()`.
RecoverIf VRF is stuck for 1 day, anyone can recover the pending prize back to the fund.

8. Secondary Market Behavior

A buyer inherits the Stone state: lock end, escrow, redeem rights, early-exit rights, weight, rarity, and Genesis metadata. Season claims that were already finalized stay with the recorded snapshot owner, so pending claim value should be priced into secondary sales.

During the final hour before a season ends, transfers revert to reduce last-minute reward sniping. Outside that lockout, active vault NFTs are transferable.

9. Utility V1 and Heat

Utility V1 keeps the core mint, lock, pool, and lottery mechanics unchanged. Heat is an off-chain, reproducible accounting layer derived from events and ownership snapshots.

The initial policy is 1 Heat per day per eligible Stone. Heat is intended for next-season perks such as allowlist tier, early mint window, discount bps, and discounted mint cap. Heat does not affect on-chain lottery weighting in V1.

10. Operations

The system is permissionless for normal operations: anyone can finalize ended seasons, request eligible prize draws, recover timed-out draws, claim season rewards, claim assigned prizes, or trigger dev withdrawal to the immutable dev address.

Mainnet deploy uses real SATO and Chainlink VRF parameters. Sepolia deploy uses mocks for testing. The contract has no pause, no upgrade admin, and no privileged reward distributor.
