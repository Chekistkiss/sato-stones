# Sato Stones — Marketing sync

## Simplified product message

- **Vault, not sale curve:** users lock SATO in NFT vaults; no bonding-curve sell on mint.
- **Supply:** 2,100 Stones max.
- **Deposit:** 100–10,000 SATO subject to dynamic cap.
- **Lock choices:** 15 / 30 / 60 days.
- **No special early-mint tier:** all Stones follow the same rules; rarity comes from locked SATO and duration weight only.
- **One reward season:** 2% mint fee and pre-finalization pool share of early-exit penalties feed a single reward pool.
- **Early exit:** burns the NFT and routes penalty to reward pool / burn / prize / dev.
- **After lock:** redeem 100% locked SATO and keep the NFT.
- **Lottery:** penalty-funded prize draws use active locked Stones as weighted tickets.

- [ ] Site says one reward season, not recurring seasons.
